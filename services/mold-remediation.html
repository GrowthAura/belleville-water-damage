Is the musty smell in a Belleville cellar mold, and does it need professional removal?

Usually yes, it is mold or mildew growing on damp wood, paper or stored items. Small surface patches can be cleaned, but growth on joists, walls or large areas needs professional removal.

A faint earthy smell in an old stone cellar is common. A strong smell that reaches the first floor, visible growth on floor joists, or white and green fuzz on stored belongings means humidity has been too high for too long.

Why mold is common in Belleville homes

Porous foundations let moisture in constantly.

Dirt-floor cellars and crawl spaces release ground moisture into the air.

Old galvanized and cast-iron plumbing develops slow leaks inside walls.

Metro East summers are humid, so basements do not dry out naturally.

Sewer backups and seepage are often mopped up without proper drying.

Rental units can sit with unreported leaks.

Mold on plaster, paneling and old wood

Older houses need a different approach from newer drywall homes.

Plaster

Plaster itself resists mold, but the paint, wallpaper and dust on it do not. Surface growth can often be cleaned. If the wood lath behind is wet and moldy, the plaster comes off.

Wood paneling and wallpaper

Basement paneling from past remodels frequently hides mold on the wall behind. It is removed to check.

Floor joists and beams

Old lumber is dense and usually sound under the surface. It is HEPA-vacuumed, scrubbed or sanded clean, and dried.

Drywall

In newer homes and remodels, moldy drywall and insulation are cut out and replaced.

Our remediation steps

Inspect and measure moisture to find the source.

Set containment with plastic sheeting and a HEPA-filtered negative air machine.

Remove contaminated porous materials in sealed bags.

Clean remaining surfaces by HEPA vacuuming and scrubbing.

Dry the area with dehumidifiers to a normal moisture level.

Rebuild walls and finishes. See Water Damaged Drywall Repair.

Keeping it from coming back

Removal lasts only if the moisture is controlled. In a cellar that means a dehumidifier that drains automatically, gutters and downspouts that carry water away from the wall, a vapor barrier over bare earth, and fixing any plumbing leak. Crawl space moisture is covered on our Crawl Space Water Removal page.

Landlords and tenants

Mold complaints in rentals are easier to resolve early. We inspect, give a written description of what was found and what caused it, and complete the work around tenant schedules. Keeping a record protects both sides.

Do you need testing?

Not usually, when mold is visible. Testing by an independent company makes sense when there is a smell with no visible source, when a real estate sale requires it, or to confirm results afterward.

Health

Mold affects people differently. Those with asthma, allergies or weakened immune systems tend to react more. Color does not indicate how harmful a mold is. Talk to a doctor about symptoms, and keep sensitive people away from the affected area until it is cleaned.

Cost and insurance

A small area is typically in the high hundreds to low thousands of dollars. A whole basement, crawl space or attic costs more. Insurance generally pays for mold only when it follows a covered water loss, and often with a cap. Basement mold removal in Belleville, IL caused by long-term dampness is usually not covered.

Mold questions

Will mold after water damage go away once things dry?

No. It goes dormant and returns with humidity. It has to be physically removed.

Is bleach enough?

Not on porous materials. It lightens the stain without removing growth inside the material.

How long does remediation take?

One to three days for small areas, a week or more for larger ones.

Is black mold cleanup different?

No. All mold is contained and removed the same way.

Can a musty basement smell be fixed without tearing out walls?

Sometimes. If the source is surface growth and high humidity, cleaning and dehumidification can be enough. Hidden growth behind finishes means opening them.
