Why are galvanized and cast-iron pipes in older Belleville homes more likely to burst in winter?

Galvanized steel rusts from the inside until the wall is thin, and cast iron cracks with age. Cold makes both brittle, and ice in a weakened pipe splits it easily.

Many older houses still have original galvanized supply lines running through unheated cellars, crawl spaces and outside walls with no insulation. They may have held for decades and then let go in a single cold week.

Shut the water off

The main valve is usually in the basement or cellar where the Illinois American Water line comes through the front wall, near the meter. Older gate valves have a round handle and may be stiff. Turn clockwise until it stops. If it will not turn or keeps leaking, the utility can shut the water off at the curb.

Then open the lowest faucet in the house to relieve pressure, and switch off the water heater.

Where pipes break in Belleville houses

In the cellar along the front wall, where cold air enters around the sill.

In crawl spaces under kitchen and bathroom additions.

Inside outside walls behind kitchen sinks.

In second-floor bathrooms above unheated porches.

At outdoor spigots left with a hose attached.

In vacant rentals where the heat was turned down or off between tenants.

What water does to an old house

Plaster

Plaster holds up to short wetting but softens and separates from the lath if it stays wet. Ceilings are the main concern because wet plaster is heavy.

Wood floors

Original strip flooring cups as the boards swell. Prompt drying can flatten it again.

Balloon framing

Many older houses have wall cavities open from basement to attic. Water from an upstairs break can run the full height of the wall and show up in the cellar.

Knob-and-tube or old wiring

Water in walls with old wiring is a fire and shock risk. Power to affected circuits stays off until an electrician clears it.

Our repair process

Extract standing water. See Emergency Water Removal.

Map moisture through walls, ceilings and floors with meters and thermal imaging.

Remove materials that cannot be saved: sagging plaster, soaked drywall, wet insulation.

Dry the structure with air movers and dehumidifiers, checked daily.

Repair plaster or drywall, trim and flooring. Ceilings are covered on our Ceiling Water Damage Repair page.

A plumber repairs the pipe. When galvanized lines have failed once, ask the plumber about the condition of the rest.

Landlords and empty units

Frozen pipe water damage in Belleville, IL is common in rentals that sit empty in January. A burst in an upstairs flat can soak the unit below before anyone notices. If you manage property, keep heat on in vacant units, shut the water off at the unit if possible, and check weekly. Our Apartment Water Damage page covers multi-unit jobs.

Thawing a frozen pipe safely

If a tap produces only a trickle in freezing weather, the line is probably frozen but not yet burst. Open the faucet, warm the pipe with a hair dryer or heating pad starting from the faucet end, and never use a torch or open flame. Know where the main valve is before you start, because the pipe may already be split and will leak once it thaws.

Insurance and cost

Sudden pipe breaks are usually covered under homeowners and landlord policies, provided reasonable heat was maintained. A break caught quickly in one room typically costs hundreds to low thousands of dollars to dry and repair. Water that ran for hours through multiple floors costs more. We supply photos, the cause and drying logs.

Burst pipe questions

A frozen pipe burst while I was at work. What first?

Shut off the main, turn off power to wet rooms if safe, and call. The crew is dispatched immediately.

Can wet plaster be dried instead of replaced?

Often, if it is still hard and attached. It is dried slowly and checked for looseness.

Is broken pipe flooding in a cellar a big deal if the cellar is unfinished?

Yes. The water soaks masonry and the floor framing above, and the damp air rises into the house.

Does an old pipe leak count as sudden damage?

A sudden break usually does. A drip that has gone on for months is often treated by insurers as gradual damage.

Do you repair the plumbing?

No. A plumber fixes the pipe. We handle the water, drying and repairs to the building.
