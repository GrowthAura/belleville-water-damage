Is standing water in a Belleville crawl space or dirt-floor cellar a problem if it drains on its own?

Yes. Even when it drains, the soil and air stay damp for weeks. That moisture rises into floor joists and subflooring, causing mold, wood rot, sagging floors and musty air upstairs.

Up to half the air on a home's first floor can come from below it. A wet crawl space is not separate from the living space.

Why Belleville homes have these spaces

Many older houses were built with a small cellar under part of the house, for the furnace and coal, and crawl space or bare earth under the rest. Kitchens, bathrooms and porches were added later over shallow crawl spaces with little ventilation. Plumbing for those additions runs through the crawl space, which puts old pipes over bare soil.

Sources of water

Rain and groundwater entering through foundation walls and vents.

Downspouts emptying next to the house.

Leaking supply and drain lines under kitchens and baths.

A cracked sewer line under the house.

Grading that slopes toward the foundation.

Condensation in summer when humid air meets cool surfaces.

Signs from upstairs

A musty smell, strongest in rooms over the crawl space. Floors that feel soft, bouncy or cold. Hardwood cupping for no obvious reason. Higher humidity indoors. Insects and rodents. If you can look in, hanging insulation, water stains on joists and white or dark growth on wood confirm it.

What we do

Pump

Standing water is pumped out with low-profile pumps that work in shallow depths.

Clear

Fallen insulation, old plastic, wet cardboard and debris are bagged and removed.

Clean

If sewage was involved, the soil surface is treated and contaminated material removed. Moldy joists are cleaned. See Mold Remediation.

Dry

Low-clearance air movers and a dehumidifier ducted into the space dry the framing. Wood moisture readings are taken until joists are back to a safe level.

Protect

A heavy vapor barrier is laid over the soil and sealed at seams, and insulation is replaced where needed.

Tight access

Some crawl spaces have 18 inches of clearance and an access hatch in a closet floor. The crew works in protective suits with compact equipment. Where there is no usable access, an opening may need to be cut, and we discuss that first.

Wood damage

Joists and beams that have been wet for years can be soft. We probe suspect areas and tell you if we find decay. Structural repairs such as sistering joists or adding supports are a carpenter's or foundation contractor's job, and we will tell you plainly when one is needed.

Dirt-floor cellars

Cellar water removal in Belleville, IL follows the same approach as crawl spaces, with more headroom. After pumping, the earth floor stays damp for a long time, so dehumidification and a vapor barrier or a proper slab make the biggest difference. For full-height basements, see Basement Flood Cleanup.

Keeping it dry afterward

Extend downspouts well away from the house. Regrade soil so it slopes away. Fix plumbing leaks. Keep a vapor barrier in place. Consider a dehumidifier made for crawl spaces. Where water enters regularly, a drainage system and sump installed by a waterproofing contractor may be the long-term answer.

Cost and insurance

Pumping and drying a small crawl space typically costs from several hundred dollars to a couple of thousand. Insulation removal, mold cleaning and a new vapor barrier add to it. Insurance covers crawl space water when the cause is a covered event such as a sudden pipe break. Groundwater and long-term moisture are generally excluded.

Crawl space questions

How much standing water in crawl space areas is too much?

Any standing water should be removed. Even a damp soil surface needs a vapor barrier.

Does crawl space moisture really affect the house above?

Yes. It raises indoor humidity, feeds mold and damages wood floors.

How long does drying take?

Three to seven days for the framing, depending on how wet the wood is.

Do you do wet crawl space cleanup after a sewer line break?

Yes. Contaminated soil surface and materials are removed and the area disinfected.

Will you tell me if the joists are rotted?

Yes. We report what we find, with photos.
