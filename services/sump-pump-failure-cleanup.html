What do you do first when the sump pump fails in a Belleville storm and the power is out?

Move valuables off the floor, unplug anything electrical in the basement from a dry spot, and call for water removal. Without power or a backup, the pit will keep filling.

If you own a generator, run it outdoors and power the pump through a properly rated cord. If the outage is short and the pit has not overflowed yet, bailing with a bucket can buy time.

A quick check when the power is on

Is the pump plugged in, and has the outlet's reset button tripped?

Has the breaker tripped?

Lift the float by hand. Does the motor start?

Is the motor humming without moving water? The impeller may be jammed or the discharge blocked.

Is water flowing out of the discharge pipe outside?

Is the pit full of gravel or debris?

If the pump will not run, it needs replacing by a plumber. We deal with the water.

Why it matters in newer Belleville homes

Homes built east and north of the older city were designed around drain tile and a sump pit. The basements are dry as long as the pump works, so builders and owners finished them with carpet, drywall and built-ins. That makes a single pump the only thing between groundwater and a finished family room. Ameren Illinois outages during summer storms are the usual trigger.

What the cleanup includes

Control the water level

We set a temporary pump in the pit so it stops overflowing while work is under way.

Extract

Water is pulled from carpet, pad, vinyl plank and concrete.

Check the walls

Baseboards come off and wall bases are metered. If water wicked into drywall, the lower section is cut out and wet insulation removed.

Clean

Groundwater from a pit carries silt and bacteria. Floors and framing are cleaned and treated.

Dry

Dehumidifiers and air movers run for three to five days with daily checks.

Repair

Pad, drywall, trim and paint are replaced.

The broader steps for lower levels are on our Basement Flood Cleanup page.

What can be saved

Carpet: often, if dried within a day or two. Pad: rarely. Vinyl plank: the planks usually survive, but they have to be lifted to dry the slab. Laminate: usually swells and is replaced. Drywall: the dry portion above the cut line stays. Furniture: solid wood and metal are fine; particleboard swells.

Before the next storm

Ask a plumber about a battery backup pump, a water-powered backup if your home has adequate city water pressure, a high-water alarm, and an annual test. Pour a bucket of water into the pit each spring to confirm the pump starts.

Sump pump questions

My sump pump failed and the basement flooded overnight. Is it too late to save the carpet?

Probably not. Call now. Carpet is usually recoverable within the first day or two.

My sump pump is not working but the floor is dry. What now?

Call a plumber today. If water appears before the pump is replaced, call us.

Is water from a sump pit overflow clean?

No. It is groundwater and is treated as gray water. Soaked pad is replaced and surfaces are cleaned.

How much does cleanup cost?

Drying takes three to five days. Repairs follow and depend on how much was removed.
