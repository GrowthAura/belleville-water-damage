Can a water-stained plaster ceiling in an older Belleville home be repaired instead of replaced?

Usually, yes. If the plaster is still hard and firmly attached to the lath, it can be dried, sealed and repainted. Soft, crumbling or sagging sections are cut out and patched.

Plaster is far more water-tolerant than drywall. The test is simple: press gently near the stain. Solid plaster does not move. Plaster that flexes or sounds hollow has lost its grip on the lath.

Danger signs

A bulge or belly in the ceiling. A crack that has opened recently. Water dripping through a light fixture. Plaster dust or chunks on the floor. Wet plaster is very heavy, and a falling section can injure someone. Clear the room, switch off the circuit and call.

Plaster versus drywall

Plaster and lath

Found in most houses built before the 1950s. It is dried slowly. Loose areas can sometimes be re-secured with plaster washers and adhesive. Missing sections are patched with plaster or with drywall shimmed level to the old surface, then skim-coated so the repair disappears.

Drywall

Found in newer homes and remodels. Saturated drywall sags and is replaced. Lightly wetted drywall can be dried.

Drop ceilings and tiles

Common in finished basements and shops. Stained tiles are replaced, and the space above is checked and dried.

Where the water comes from

Roof leaks at flashing, valleys, chimneys and worn shingles, especially on steep older roofs.

Bathrooms above: old toilets with failed seals, cast-iron tubs with leaking drains, corroded supply lines.

Radiator and boiler piping in homes with hot-water heat.

Attic air handlers with blocked condensate lines.

Frozen pipes. See Burst Pipe Water Damage.

In two-flats and apartments, the unit upstairs.

How we repair it

Protect floors and furnishings.

Release trapped water safely.

Remove what cannot be saved and any wet insulation.

Dry the joists, lath and underside of the floor or roof above.

Repair the ceiling: plaster patch or new drywall.

Skim, sand, prime with stain blocker and paint.

Old paint

Homes built before 1978 may have lead-based paint on ceilings and trim. Where old painted plaster has to be disturbed, lead-safe work practices are used: containment, wet methods and HEPA cleanup.

Roof leaks

Roof leak water damage in Belleville, IL has two parts. A roofer stops the leak. We dry the attic framing and insulation, treat any mold on the sheathing and restore the ceiling below. Doing the interior before the roof is fixed wastes the repair.

Leaks between units

When the source is the apartment above, access and responsibility become questions. We document where the water came from and what it damaged in each unit. See Apartment Water Damage.

Cost and insurance

A small repair with a clear source usually falls in the hundreds to low thousands of dollars. Plaster restoration over a large area, high ceilings and decorative details cost more. Sudden leaks and storm damage are typically covered. Wear and long-term seepage are not.

Ceiling questions

Is a water stain on ceiling plaster always an active leak?

No. Some are old. A moisture meter tells us whether it is wet now.

My sagging plaster ceiling has been like that for years. Is it water?

It may be age. Plaster keys break over time. If there is staining as well, water is involved.

Do you do roof leak cleanup in the attic?

Yes. Wet insulation is removed and the framing dried.

Can you match ornate plaster?

Simple textures and flat ceilings, yes. Decorative medallions and cornices may need a plaster specialist.

How long does it take?

Drying takes three to five days. Repair and paint take a few more.
