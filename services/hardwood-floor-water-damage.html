Can the original hardwood floors in a Belleville bungalow be saved after a leak?

In most cases, yes. Old solid oak and pine are thick and dense, and they recover well if drying starts within a few days and is done slowly from both sides.

These floors were laid as solid three-quarter-inch boards, often with enough thickness left for several more sandings. That gives far more room for repair than modern engineered products.

How old floors are built

Strip flooring nailed to diagonal plank subfloor, not plywood. Gaps between subfloor planks let air through, which helps drying from the cellar side. There is often no vapor barrier beneath. Some rooms have pine or fir instead of oak, which is softer and marks more easily. Later finishes of shellac or varnish turn white when wet but can be sanded out.

What water does

Cupping: board edges rise as the underside swells.

White haze: moisture trapped in old finish.

Black stains: water reacted with iron and tannin in oak, often around nails or where a pet bowl or plant sat.

Buckling: boards lift when swelling has nowhere to go.

Loose boards and squeaks after drying.

Our drying method

Extract surface water immediately.

Record moisture in the boards and the subfloor.

Seal drying mats over the affected area and draw moisture through the seams.

Dry from below when the floor is over a cellar or crawl space, with airflow across the subfloor.

Keep dehumidification steady and gentle. Old wood dried too fast splits.

Monitor until the boards match unaffected rooms.

Sand and refinish once stable, usually a few weeks later.

When the floor is over a cellar

A flooded or damp cellar raises moisture in the floor above. Floors can cup days after a basement event without ever being wet on top. Drying the cellar is part of saving the floor. See Crawl Space Water Removal.

Replacing boards

Boards that buckled or rotted are replaced. Matching matters. Old flooring was often a different width and cut from old-growth timber with tighter grain. Reclaimed boards of the same species and width give the closest match, and the room is refinished as a whole.

Carpet and other floors

Wet carpet drying in Belleville, IL follows the usual rule: extract, remove the pad, dry the floor beneath, replace the pad and re-stretch. Carpet laid over old hardwood should be lifted, because it traps moisture against the wood. Laminate swells and is replaced. Sheet vinyl and tile are checked for water underneath.

Causes

Radiator and plumbing leaks, appliance lines, described on Water Heater Leak Cleanup, overflowing fixtures, roof leaks running down walls, and damp rising from below.

Cost and insurance

Drying a wood floor in one room typically runs from the high hundreds to the low thousands of dollars. Board replacement and refinishing add to it. Sudden leaks are generally covered. Water damaged floor repair in Belleville, IL caused by long-term damp is usually not.

Floor questions

Will cupping hardwood floors flatten by themselves?

Partly, if the moisture source is removed. Professional drying brings them down faster and more evenly.

Can black stains be sanded out?

Light ones, yes. Deep stains go through the board, and those boards are replaced.

Is wet subfloor drying harder with plank subfloors?

It is usually easier, because planks breathe.

Do you do carpet water extraction in rentals?

Yes.

When can the floor be refinished?

When moisture readings have been stable for a couple of weeks.
