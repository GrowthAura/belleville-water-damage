Does homeowners insurance in Illinois cover sewer backup or mine subsidence damage in Belleville?

Not by default. Sewer backup requires an added endorsement. Mine subsidence is a separate coverage offered on Illinois policies in affected counties. Standard policies mainly cover sudden, accidental water damage.

We are a restoration crew, not agents or adjusters. This is general information, and your own policy governs.

The four coverages that matter here

Standard homeowners coverage

Sudden and accidental discharge: burst pipes, failed water heaters, appliance hoses, fixture overflows, and rain through storm-damaged roofing.

Sewer and drain backup endorsement

Covers water and sewage that comes up through drains or overflows a sump. In Belleville's older neighborhoods this is the most valuable add-on. It carries its own limit.

Flood insurance

Covers rising surface water, such as creek flooding and street flooding entering the home. Bought separately.

Mine subsidence insurance

In St. Clair County, insurers include mine subsidence coverage on residential policies unless the owner declines it in writing. It covers structural damage from the ground settling over old mine workings, handled through the Illinois Mine Subsidence Insurance Fund. It does not cover water damage as such. If subsidence breaks a water or sewer line, different parts of the loss may fall under different coverages.

What is usually not covered

Groundwater seeping through an old foundation. Long-term leaks from corroded pipes. Wear and deferred maintenance. Mold unrelated to a covered loss. The cost of replacing the failed pipe or appliance itself.

First-day checklist

Stop the water and limit the damage. This is required by most policies.

Take photos and video before moving anything.

Report the claim and note the claim number.

Keep receipts.

Do not discard damaged items until documented.

Begin water removal and drying without waiting for the adjuster.

That is the water damage claim documentation adjusters rely on. In older homes we also note pre-existing conditions honestly, such as long-standing seepage marks, so the new damage is clearly separated from the old.

Old houses and claim disputes

Adjusters sometimes question whether damage in an older home is sudden or long-term. Clear evidence helps: the failed fitting, fresh water lines, readings that show recent saturation. We record these at the start, because they cannot be recreated later.

Claim questions

Is water damage covered by insurance when an old pipe finally breaks?

A sudden break usually is. Damage from slow corrosion leaks over time often is not.

Is sewer backup coverage expensive?

It is generally a modest addition to the premium. Ask your agent.

Does mine subsidence cover a cracked basement wall that leaks?

It may cover the structural damage if subsidence is confirmed. The fund investigates the cause.

Should I wait for the adjuster?

No. Start emergency work and document it.

Can you meet the adjuster?

Yes, on site or by sending the file.
