Who calls for cleanup when a Belleville rental floods: the tenant or the landlord?

The tenant should stop the water if possible and notify the landlord at once. The landlord or property manager normally arranges cleanup of the building. Either one can call us to get a crew started.

In an emergency, speed matters more than who dials. If you are a tenant and cannot reach the owner, call, and we will help you make the unit safe and contact the landlord for authorization on building repairs.

For tenants

Shut off the water at the fixture or the unit valve if you can.

Move your belongings out of the water.

Tell the landlord in writing as well as by phone, with photos.

Your belongings are generally covered by your own renters insurance, not the landlord's policy.

Do not remove flooring or walls yourself. That is the owner's property.

Leaks between units

The classic case is an upstairs unit leaking into the one below: an overflowing tub, a washer hose, a failed supply line. Both units need attention. Upstairs, the floor and subfloor. Downstairs, the ceiling, walls and floor. We record each unit separately, because different insurance policies may respond.

Military tenants and short tenancies

With Scott Air Force Base nearby, many renters are on fixed-length postings and may be away on duty when a leak happens. Vacant periods between tenants are also when frozen pipes and slow leaks go unnoticed. If you manage units through these gaps, keep the heat on, check weekly and consider leak alarms.

Rental questions

My apartment flooded from upstairs. Who pays?

The building owner's insurance usually covers the structure. Your renters policy covers your belongings. Fault between tenants is a matter for the insurers.

Can a tenant authorize work?

A tenant can authorize emergency steps to stop damage. Repairs to the building need the owner's approval.

Is tenant water damage the tenant's cost?

Only where the tenant caused it through negligence, and that is decided by the lease and insurers, not by us.

Do you handle duplex water damage where both sides are affected?

Yes, with separate records for each side.

Can you work with my property management company?

Yes. We report to whoever the owner designates.
