What should a Belleville homeowner do when the water heater tank splits in the basement?

Close the cold-water valve above the heater, turn off its gas or its breaker, keep clear of water near electrical items, and call for cleanup. If the valve is stuck, shut the main.

In many older homes the heater sits near the floor drain and most of the water finds its way out. The trouble starts when the drain is slow or blocked, or the heater is in a finished part of a newer basement.

Why tanks fail

Minerals in the water settle at the bottom and overheat the steel. The protective anode rod wears out. Rust eats through from inside. Tanks older than ten years are on borrowed time. Rusty water, rumbling sounds and dampness around the base are the warnings.

Appliance leaks, room by room

Basement laundry

Washer hoses burst and standpipes overflow when the old drain line is partly clogged. Water spreads across the floor to stored items and the base of the stairs.

Kitchen

Dishwasher and ice-maker lines leak under cabinets. In houses with original wood floors under later layers of vinyl, water gets trapped between layers.

Upstairs bathrooms and laundry closets

In two-flats and remodeled homes, an upstairs washer or supply line sends water into the unit or room below.

Boilers and radiators

Homes with hot-water heat can leak at radiator valves and boiler fittings.

Layers of old flooring

Kitchens in older houses can have three or four layers: wood, linoleum, plywood, vinyl. Water between them does not evaporate. We test through the layers and lift what is needed to dry the original floor. See Hardwood Floor Water Damage.

Clean water or not

Supply-line and tank water is clean at first. Water from a washer drain or dishwasher is gray water with detergent and food waste. Any water that sat more than two days is treated as contaminated because bacteria multiply in it.

Rentals

Appliance leaks are among the most frequent calls from landlords. Washing machine overflow cleanup in Belleville, IL in an upstairs unit usually involves the ceiling of the unit below. See Apartment Water Damage.

Cost and insurance

Appliance leak water damage in Belleville, IL limited to one room generally costs in the hundreds to low thousands of dollars. Damage across floors is more. Sudden failures are normally covered by homeowners and landlord policies. The appliance itself and long-running leaks usually are not.

Prevention

Replace rubber washer hoses with braided steel. Install a pan and alarm under the heater. Keep the floor drain clear. Know the age of the tank. Shut off the washer valves when you travel.

Leak questions

My water heater burst overnight. Is the basement ruined?

Rarely. Unfinished basements mainly need extraction, cleaning and drying.

Can dishwasher leak water damage reach the basement ceiling?

Yes. It often shows as a stain below the kitchen.

How would I notice a refrigerator water line leak?

Soft flooring near the fridge, a stain below, or a musty odor.

Do you remove the old water heater?

No. A plumber replaces it. We handle the water damage.

How long does drying take?

Two to five days, more with layered floors.
