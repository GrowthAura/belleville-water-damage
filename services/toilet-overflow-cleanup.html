Is a toilet overflow in a Belleville home with one bathroom an emergency call?

Yes, when water has spread beyond the bathroom, soaked the floor or contains waste. With only one bathroom, the household cannot function until it is cleaned, disinfected and the drain is cleared.

Many older homes here have a single upstairs bathroom directly over the dining room or kitchen. An overflow there reaches the ceiling below within minutes.

Stop it

Close the valve on the wall or floor behind the toilet by turning it clockwise.

If the valve is seized, common on old fixtures, lift the tank lid and raise the float arm or press the flapper shut.

Do not flush.

If the tub or basement drain is also backing up, the main line is blocked. Stop using water and see Sewer Backup Cleanup.

Why older toilets and drains overflow

Cast-iron drain pipes build up scale and narrow over decades. Clay sewer laterals let in roots. Old toilets with worn parts run and overfill. Wipes, paper towels and hygiene products that newer plumbing might pass will lodge in an old line. A second overflow soon after the first means the line needs a plumber's camera, not just a plunger.

How dirty is the water?

Tank or supply water

Clean. Dried like any supply leak.

Bowl water without waste

Gray water. Flooring is cleaned and often lifted.

Water with waste or from the drain

Category 3, the most contaminated. Porous materials are removed and all surfaces disinfected.

Old bathroom floors

Older bathrooms often have small ceramic tile set in a thick mortar bed over plank subfloor, or layers of vinyl over wood. Water gets under the edges and around the toilet flange. A mortar-bed floor can hold moisture for a long time. We test the floor and the ceiling below, and lift flooring only where readings show it is necessary.

The ceiling underneath

Plaster ceilings below a bathroom show damage as stains, cracks or sagging. Wet plaster contaminated by waste water is removed. Clean-water wetting can often be dried. See Ceiling Water Damage Repair.

Our cleanup

Extract water in the bathroom and adjoining rooms.

Remove contaminated carpet, pad, vinyl or wet ceiling material.

Pull the toilet when water has gone beneath the flooring.

Wash with detergent, then disinfect.

Dry the subfloor, wall bases and ceiling cavity.

Repair ceiling, walls and floor. Wood floors in the hall are covered on Hardwood Floor Water Damage.

Rentals

In a rental, tell the landlord immediately. Overflows in upper flats affect the unit below, and both need checking.

Cost and insurance

A contained overflow typically costs in the hundreds of dollars to clean and dry. Bathroom water damage in Belleville, IL that involves the ceiling below and new flooring reaches the low thousands. Sudden overflows from a fixture are usually covered. Backups of the main sewer need a backup endorsement.

Overflow questions

My toilet overflowed for only a minute. Do I need a crew?

If it stayed on a hard floor and you dried it at once, probably not. If it reached carpet, went under baseboards or shows below, yes.

Does a clogged toilet flood mean the sewer line is bad?

Not always. Repeated clogs or several fixtures backing up suggest it.

How long does bathroom flood cleanup take?

Cleaning is done the same day. Drying takes two to five days.

Will the bathroom be usable during drying?

Usually, once the toilet is reset and the drain is clear.

Do you fix the toilet?

No. A plumber does. We clean, disinfect and dry.
