Which water damage service does a Belleville home actually need: extraction, drying or repair?

Usually all three, in that order. Water is removed first, the structure is dried for several days, then damaged plaster, drywall and flooring are repaired. Sewage adds a cleaning and disinfection step.

Mitigation is the emergency half of the work: stopping the spread, removing water and drying. Restoration is the rebuilding half. Hiring a water mitigation company that also repairs means one schedule and one point of contact.
