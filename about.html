Why older homes need a different approach

Belleville has thousands of houses with limestone and brick foundations, plaster walls, plank subfloors and original wood floors. They cannot be dried the way a new drywall house is. Masonry is dried gradually. Sound plaster is saved, not torn out. Old trim is removed with care and put back. Knowing the difference keeps the character of the house and often lowers the cost.
